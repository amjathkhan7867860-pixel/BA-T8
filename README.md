# E-Commerce Sales Data Visualization

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an **E-Commerce Sales Dataset** using **Tableau Public**. The objective is to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, category performance, and regional sales trends through interactive visualizations and dashboards.

The dataset contains **3,500 records** with information about order dates, products, categories, regions, quantity, sales, and profit.

### Dataset Details

| Field        | Description                        |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Order Date   | Date on which the order was placed |
| Product Name | Name of the product                |
| Category     | Product category                   |
| Region       | Sales region                       |
| Quantity     | Number of products sold            |
| Sales        | Total sales amount                 |
| Profit       | Profit generated from the order    |

**Data Period:** January 2022 – December 2024

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the best-performing product categories.
* Compare sales performance across regions.
* Analyze product-level sales performance.
* Understand sales trends over time.
* Create an interactive Tableau dashboard.
* Provide business recommendations based on the analysis.

---

# 📈 Visualizations Created

## 1. Sales by Category – Bar Chart

This visualization compares total sales across different product categories.

**Purpose:**
To identify which categories contribute the most to overall sales.

**Key observation:**
Electronics generated the highest sales, followed by Accessories and Office products.

---

## 2. Sales by Region – Bar Chart

This visualization compares sales performance across the four regions:

* North
* South
* East
* West

**Purpose:**
To identify the strongest and weakest geographical markets.

**Key observation:**
The West region generated the highest sales, while the North region generated the lowest sales.

---

## 3. Sales and Profit Trend Over Time – Line Chart

A line chart is used to analyze sales and profit performance over the period from 2022 to 2024.

**Purpose:**
To understand changes in business performance over time and identify periods of growth or decline.

---

## 4. Product Performance – Bar Chart

This visualization compares products based on their total sales and/or profit.

Products analyzed include:

* Camera
* Monitor
* Printer
* Mouse
* Smartphone
* Tablet
* Laptop
* Keyboard
* Headphones
* Other products in the dataset

**Purpose:**
To identify high-performing products and products that may require additional attention.

# 🔍 Key Findings

## Finding 1 – Electronics is the Leading Category

Electronics generated approximately **₹5.33 million in sales**, making it the highest-performing category.

Accessories generated approximately **₹4.25 million**, while Office products generated approximately **₹1.09 million**.

This shows that Electronics is the major contributor to overall sales.

---

## Finding 2 – West is the Top-Performing Region

The **West region** generated approximately **₹2.84 million in sales**, making it the highest-performing region.

The North region generated approximately **₹2.49 million**, making it the lowest among the four regions.

This indicates an opportunity to improve sales performance in the North region.

---

## Finding 3 – Camera and Monitor are Strong Products

Among the products, **Camera** generated approximately **₹1.18 million in sales**, while **Monitor** generated approximately **₹1.16 million**.

These products are among the strongest contributors to overall sales and could be prioritized for inventory and marketing strategies.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

## Recommendation 1 – Focus on High-Performing Electronics

Since Electronics contributes the largest share of sales, the business should:

* Maintain sufficient inventory.
* Promote high-performing electronics.
* Create product bundles.
* Use targeted marketing campaigns.
* Monitor demand regularly.

This can help maintain the category's strong performance.

---

## Recommendation 2 – Improve Sales in the North Region

Since North has the lowest regional sales, the business should investigate the reasons for lower performance.

Possible strategies include:

* Region-specific promotions.
* Discount campaigns.
* Targeted digital advertising.
* Improved product availability.
* Customer-focused offers.

These strategies could help increase sales in the North region.

---

# 📝 Overall Conclusion

The e-commerce sales analysis provides useful insights into the company's sales and profitability performance.

The analysis shows that **Electronics is the strongest category**, while the **West region is the top-performing market**. Products such as **Camera and Monitor** also contribute significantly to overall sales.

The interactive Tableau dashboard makes it easier to explore these results using category, region, and date filters. Based on the findings, the company should continue investing in high-performing products and categories while developing targeted strategies to improve weaker regions.

Overall, the dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of e-commerce performance and can support better **sales planning, inventory management, marketing decisions, and business strategy**.
